About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on building professionals capable of managing digital, cyber and technology risk in complex and highly regulated environments.


Working in collaboration with academic partners including UK universities, IDR bridges the gap between theoretical research and real-world practice. Participants gain practical experience through applied projects, risk modelling and framework implementation.

Our Model

Academy

Training programs and bootcamps designed to equip students and professionals with practical cyber and digital risk capabilities.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing new risk models, AI governance frameworks and digital security solutions through applied research and experimentation.

Advisory Services

Supporting organisations in implementing frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2 to strengthen digital risk management.
